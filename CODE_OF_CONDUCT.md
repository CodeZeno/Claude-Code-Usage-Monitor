# Code of Conduct

## Our community

We welcome contributors and users of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Everyone participating in this project's issues, pull requests, discussions,
and other community spaces is expected to treat others with respect, regardless
of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and open to questions and constructive feedback.
- Discuss ideas and code respectfully, including when disagreeing.
- Respect personal boundaries and the privacy of other participants.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and adjust behaviour when asked.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualised comments or
unwanted advances, and publishing someone's private information without consent
are unacceptable. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is also
unacceptable.

These expectations apply in project spaces and when representing the project
publicly.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to
[contact@codezeno.com.au](mailto:contact@codezeno.com.au) with the subject
`[Conduct] Claude Code Usage Monitor`. Include a description, relevant links or
redacted evidence, and a way to contact you. Do not post personal information in
a public issue. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports with discretion and share identifying details
only as needed to investigate and address the concern. A maintainer involved in
a report should step aside from reviewing it where another maintainer is
available.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, issue a warning, restrict
participation temporarily, or permanently exclude participants, depending on the
severity and repetition of the behavior. Where practical, they will explain the
reason for an action privately to the affected participant. Requests to review
an enforcement decision can be sent to the same contact address.
